Infrastructure Engineer, Grid Operations Platform

Example application form for testing Penates. Fictional company. No data is submitted.

Who we are

Northwind Grid builds the control and monitoring software that utilities use to run battery storage sites and substations. Our platform ingests telemetry from 400+ field sites, runs dispatch decisions every four seconds, and has to keep working when a site's cellular link drops. We are a 300-person company; about half of us are field engineers and commissioning technicians.

The team

The Grid Operations Platform team (9 engineers) owns the cloud side (AWS, Kafka, TimescaleDB, Kubernetes) and the edge side: ruggedized Linux gateways at each site that buffer telemetry and run local failsafes. You will split your time roughly 70/30 between cloud infrastructure and edge fleet management.

What you will do

  • Run and harden our Kubernetes platform and the Kafka pipeline that carries site telemetry.
  • Own the build, patching and remote update process for 1,200 edge gateways running Yocto Linux.
  • Work with commissioning engineers when a new battery site comes online: network cutover, certificate enrollment, gateway acceptance tests.
  • Write and review code. Our infrastructure tooling is Python and Rust; we expect every engineer to ship code weekly.
  • Take part in site visits about four times a year (travel 10 percent, US only).

Requirements

  • 5+ years in infrastructure or platform engineering.
  • Production Kubernetes and Terraform.
  • Linux at the systems level: systemd, networking, kernel parameters, embedded or appliance Linux a strong plus.
  • Comfort with OT/ICS concepts (SCADA, DNP3, Modbus) or willingness to learn fast.
  • Python required; Rust a plus.
  • NERC CIP experience is a plus.

Compensation

$140,000 to $165,000 base, annual bonus, equity. Remote within the US with quarterly site travel. Candidates in Texas or Arizona preferred because most sites are there.

Interview process

A 30-minute recruiter call, a 60-minute technical conversation with two engineers (no live coding; we walk through a system you have run), a take-home exercise of no more than three hours, and a final round with the hiring manager and a cross-functional partner. We aim to make a decision within three weeks of your first call.

Benefits

  • Medical, dental and vision with 90 percent of premiums covered for you and 75 percent for dependents.
  • Retirement plan with company match, vested immediately.
  • Flexible PTO with a 15-day minimum, plus 11 company holidays and a winter break.
  • Home office stipend of $1,000 at hire and $500 per year after.
  • Paid parental leave of 16 weeks for all parents.
