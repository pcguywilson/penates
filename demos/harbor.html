About Harbor Federal Systems

Harbor Federal Systems is a mid-size government contractor supporting defense and civilian agencies with cloud migration, DevSecOps and cyber operations. We hold an ISO 27001 certification and CMMC Level 2.

The program

You will support a classified analytics program moving workloads from on-premises data centers into AWS GovCloud and AWS Secret Region. Day-to-day work happens in the unclassified environment from home; roughly 20 percent of the time you will need to work on-site at a customer facility in Chantilly, VA to access classified systems.

Responsibilities

  • Build and maintain infrastructure as code (Terraform and CloudFormation) for multi-account AWS GovCloud landing zones.
  • Automate security controls: Config rules, GuardDuty, Security Hub, SCPs, and STIG hardening of AMIs.
  • Support ATO packages: produce evidence for NIST 800-53 Rev 5 controls and work POA&Ms with the ISSO.
  • Operate EKS and Rancher RKE2 clusters on the low side.
  • Write automation in Python and PowerShell.

Required

  • Active TS/SCI clearance with CI polygraph (required on day one; we cannot sponsor).
  • DoD 8140 IAT Level II certification (Security+ CE or higher) within 30 days of start.
  • 7+ years of systems or cloud engineering, 4+ on AWS.
  • Terraform in production; CloudFormation a plus.

Nice to have

  • AWS Certified Security - Specialty.
  • Experience with AWS Secret or Top Secret regions.
  • Ansible, Packer, GitLab CI.

Salary range $145,000 to $190,000 depending on clearance and experience. Equal Opportunity Employer, including veterans and individuals with disabilities.

Interview process

A 30-minute recruiter call, a 60-minute technical conversation with two engineers (no live coding; we walk through a system you have run), a take-home exercise of no more than three hours, and a final round with the hiring manager and a cross-functional partner. We aim to make a decision within three weeks of your first call.

Benefits

  • Medical, dental and vision with 90 percent of premiums covered for you and 75 percent for dependents.
  • Retirement plan with company match, vested immediately.
  • Flexible PTO with a 15-day minimum, plus 11 company holidays and a winter break.
  • Home office stipend of $1,000 at hire and $500 per year after.
  • Paid parental leave of 16 weeks for all parents.
