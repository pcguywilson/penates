About Lattice Labs

Lattice builds internal developer platforms for mid-size software companies: golden-path templates, preview environments, and an AI review assistant that comments on pull requests. 60 people, Series B, fully remote across US time zones.

How we work

Every engineer here uses coding agents daily (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot). We care less about which tool and more about judgment: knowing what to delegate, how to review generated changes, and how to keep a codebase coherent when agents touch it. We write design docs for anything over a week of work and we ship behind feature flags.

The role

  • Own the platform that runs our customers' preview environments (Kubernetes, Argo CD, Crossplane, AWS and GCP).
  • Build the evaluation harness for our AI review assistant: test corpora, regression gates, cost tracking.
  • Design features end to end: requirement, design doc, implementation, rollout, metrics.
  • Pair with customers' platform teams during onboarding.

You might be a fit if you have

  • Built something real with LLMs or agents, beyond a chat window.
  • Strong Go or TypeScript (most of our code is Go).
  • Kubernetes operators or controllers in production.
  • GCP experience (half our customers are on GKE).
  • Product sense: you can explain why a feature matters, not only how it works.

Pay

$170,000 to $205,000 plus equity. This is a contract-to-hire role for the first six months.

Interview process

A 30-minute recruiter call, a 60-minute technical conversation with two engineers (no live coding; we walk through a system you have run), a take-home exercise of no more than three hours, and a final round with the hiring manager and a cross-functional partner. We aim to make a decision within three weeks of your first call.

Benefits

  • Medical, dental and vision with 90 percent of premiums covered for you and 75 percent for dependents.
  • Retirement plan with company match, vested immediately.
  • Flexible PTO with a 15-day minimum, plus 11 company holidays and a winter break.
  • Home office stipend of $1,000 at hire and $500 per year after.
  • Paid parental leave of 16 weeks for all parents.
