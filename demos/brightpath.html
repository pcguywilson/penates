Our mission

BrightPath Health Alliance is a nonprofit network of 40 community health centers serving uninsured and underinsured patients across Maryland, Virginia and the District. Every dollar we save on technology goes back into patient care.

About the role

The Systems Engineer III keeps our clinics' technology running: the Epic-adjacent integration servers, a Microsoft 365 and Entra ID tenant with 2,800 users, Intune-managed laptops and tablets, and a small AWS footprint for our data warehouse. You will be one of three engineers on the infrastructure team.

This is a hybrid role. We expect three days a week in our Washington, DC office (Navy Yard) and occasional clinic visits. Remote-only candidates will not be considered.

Responsibilities

  • Administer Entra ID, Intune, Exchange Online and Defender for Endpoint.
  • Maintain Windows Server 2019/2022 and RHEL systems, patching and backups.
  • Support HIPAA security risk assessments and incident response.
  • Lead our annual disaster recovery test.
  • Volunteer: each staff member gives two days a year to a clinic health fair.

Requirements

  • 5+ years of systems administration, Windows and Linux.
  • Deep Intune and Entra ID experience (3+ years Intune).
  • HIPAA experience.
  • Strong communication with non-technical clinical staff.

Compensation

$98,000 to $112,000, 403(b) with 5 percent match, generous PTO.

Interview process

A 30-minute recruiter call, a 60-minute technical conversation with two engineers (no live coding; we walk through a system you have run), a take-home exercise of no more than three hours, and a final round with the hiring manager and a cross-functional partner. We aim to make a decision within three weeks of your first call.

Benefits

  • Medical, dental and vision with 90 percent of premiums covered for you and 75 percent for dependents.
  • Retirement plan with company match, vested immediately.
  • Flexible PTO with a 15-day minimum, plus 11 company holidays and a winter break.
  • Home office stipend of $1,000 at hire and $500 per year after.
  • Paid parental leave of 16 weeks for all parents.
